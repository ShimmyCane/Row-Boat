3. Key Variables & Extension Guide

Variable / FileDefault / TypeWhat it Does & How to Tweak
scenario.jsonJSON ObjectTo add a new scenario, duplicate _template_example in scenario.json and set title, capacity, characters, and conflicts. The app automatically creates a top tab button for it.
scn.conflictsArray of RulesSupports direct rules (predatorId, victimId, protectorId) and role-based owner rules (ownerAbsent: true).
.wrap-rowFlexbox with wrapEnsures form fields and Add buttons in the custom builder wrap smoothly on iPads and narrower sidebars without being cut off.
