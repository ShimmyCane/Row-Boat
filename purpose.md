Purpose
Animate row boat problems

Context
There is a river that needs to be crossed, with a row boat that can only take so many people/ animal/ things. How many turns will it take to cross, and what would it look like?

Restraints
- Humans are needed to manuever the boat
- Sometimes animals will act out if humans aren't around. or if their human isn't around

Scenarios
    1) Man, Wolf, Goat, Cabbage. Wolf and Goat will listen to man, but in the absense of man, Wolf will eat Goat and Goat will eat Cabbage. Boat has two seats.
    2) Three men (M1, M2, M3) and their dogs (D1, D2, D3). Dogs will bite men if their owner isn't present. Dogs are happy with each other. Boat has three seats.
    3) Custom, allow the user to add in their characters and their relationships.
