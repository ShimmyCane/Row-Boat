Purpose
Animate row boat problems so the crossing is easy to follow.

Context
A river must be crossed in a row boat that carries only so many people, animals or things.
How many trips will it take, and what does each crossing look like?

Goals for V3
- Fit on iPhone and iPad screens, in portrait and landscape, without scrolling the page.
- Make each crossing easy to see: the boat moves across the river and rows.
- Show each landing: passengers step off one at a time, and the last one ashore is highlighted.

Restraints
- Humans are needed to maneuver the boat.
- Animals may act out if humans, or their own human, are not present.

Scenarios
1. Wolf, Goat & Cabbage. Boat holds 2.
2. 3 Men & 3 Dogs. Boat holds 3.
3. 3 Men & 3 Monsters. Boat holds 2.
4. Jealous Couples. Boat holds 2.
5. Monks & Chief. Boat holds 2.
6. Torch & Bridge (night). Boat holds 2 people plus the torch. Timed.
7. Custom. Players add characters and danger rules.
